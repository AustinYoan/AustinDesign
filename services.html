STANDBY SUPPORT備援方案

Not short-handed now?
Get ready anyway.現在不缺人，
也可以先準備好。

The next resignation is a matter of when, not if. With Standby, we already know your files when it happens, so we can step in the same day.下一次人員異動遲早會來。有了備援方案，那一天來的時候，我們已經熟悉你的圖檔，當天就能接手。

INCLUDES包含

  • A small amount of drawing edits each month每月處理少量圖面修改
  • Ongoing familiarity with your files and products持續熟悉你的圖檔與產品
  • Priority start for Bridge Support when someone leaves有人離職時，優先啟動銜接支援

A Single Project or Bridge Support can switch straight to Standby when it ends.單案委託或銜接支援結束後，也可以直接轉為備援。
